Dice

A calm library for your emulator games on GNOME.

Install
  • Install with GNOME Software Opens your app store; updates arrive like any other app

Dice shows your ROM folder as a shelf of box art and launches each game in the emulator you already use. No renaming folders, no scraper accounts, no fullscreen TV mode.

It finds your GBA, DS, 3DS, PS1, PSP and PS2 games — more systems are coming — and never changes your files, so removing it leaves your collection exactly as it was.

How Dice compares

Questions

Which systems does Dice support?

Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS, Nintendo 3DS, PlayStation, PSP and PlayStation 2. More systems are coming.

Do I have to rename or reorganise my folders?

No. Folder names like “PS2”, “PlayStation 2” or “N3DS” help, but Dice also reads each game's own header, so it can tell a PS1 disc from a PS2 or PSP one even in a mixed folder.

Where does the box art come from?

From images next to your ROMs, the art inside PSP discs, or the libretro thumbnail collection, matched by name. No account needed. You can also set any image as a game's cover.

Which emulators does it work with?

It finds mGBA, melonDS, Azahar, DuckStation, PPSSPP and PCSX2 on its own, whether they're Flatpaks or installed from your distribution, and can run any other emulator with a command you set. Emulators are installed separately, for example from Flathub.

Does Dice change my files?

No. It only reads your ROMs to find games and covers, and keeps its library, covers and settings in its own folder. Removing Dice leaves your collection exactly as it was.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Move through the grid, open a game and press Play with a gamepad.

Does Dice come with games?

No. Dice doesn't include, download or link to any games. It organises and launches backups of games you own.

Why does GNOME Software list broad permissions?

Dice starts your emulators, which are usually Flatpaks too, so it asks Flatpak to run them and to let them see your game folder. It reads your files to find games and covers, and never changes or uploads them.

Why isn't Dice on Flathub?

Flathub doesn't accept new apps made with AI assistance, and Dice was built with help from Claude. It's published from its own signed repository instead, and updates arrive through GNOME Software or flatpak update like any other app.

How do I install or remove it from a terminal?

To install:

flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists dice \
    https://drvonmiau.github.io/dice/index.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user dice io.github.drvonmiau.Dice

To remove Dice and its repository:

flatpak uninstall --user io.github.drvonmiau.Dice
flatpak remote-delete --user dice
